1.  Go to the form view of an existing employee and click on the
    "Documents" tab icon, a hierarchy of folders and files linked to that
    record will be created.
2.  Create a new employee. A hierarchy of folders and files linked to
    that record will be created.
3.  If the option *Show DMS Documents to logged employees* is enabled in
    *Employees \> Configuration \> Settings*, employees can see their
    documents in the "Documents" tab of their own public profile.
